Mission

From flight sims to competition mats — we teach aerospace engineering through hands-on drone technology.

Aero Ravens meets in Room F101 at Canyon Crest Academy, where members learn aerospace engineering and drone technology hands-on. Most of the year goes into practicing and prepping for the Aerial Drone Competition (ADC), the national circuit our team competes in every season.

Beyond competition, we've partnered with Robolink — the company behind our educational drones — to help build lessons and test new prototypes before they reach classrooms. We've also given back to the community by running a paid summer drone camp. Proceeds from the camp and from our school's fundraising event go toward equipment and supplies for the upcoming season.

Membership

Fly with us.
